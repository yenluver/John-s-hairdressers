About the salon

A proper neighbourhood salon, run properly.

Hair By John Joseph is a welcoming local hairdresser on Long Lane, offering reliable, personal hairdressing for the whole family. No corporate script, no rush — just an experienced stylist who takes the time to get your cut right.

Clients keep coming back for the same reason: it feels like your local. You'll be looked after by an award-recognised stylist who knows the neighbourhood as well as they know hair, and every appointment is unhurried and genuinely friendly from the moment you sit down.

  • Experienced, hands-on stylist for every appointment

  • Trusted by families across L19 and the wider area
